Course overview

See the structure behind the symbols.

Linear algebra is the language of systems, dimensions, and transformations. We will move from solving equations to understanding the spaces and maps beneath them.

The course covers linear systems; vectors in Rn; independence and bases; linear transformations; matrices and determinants; eigenvalues and eigenvectors; and geometric aspects of linear algebra.

Learning objectives

By the end of the course, you will be able to…

  1. 01

    Solve and analyze systems of linear equations by row reduction.

  2. 02

    Use vectors, matrix algebra, independence, and the Invertible Matrix Theorem.

  3. 03

    Represent linear transformations by matrices and interpret them geometrically.

  4. 04

    Use subspaces, bases, dimension, rank, and determinants to analyze matrices.

  5. 05

    Find eigenvalues and eigenvectors, test diagonalizability, and construct orthogonal projections.
