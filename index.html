I strongly support human mathematics, that is, mathematics understood by humans.

Human mathematicians should play the dominant role in socializing mathematics, even if AI becomes better at solving mathematical problems.

2

Mathematics exists in nature. It includes many categories, but human mathematics is the most important.

Read details
Details

Other categories include, but are not limited to, mathematics generated by large language models and Lean-verified mathematics. Mathematics becomes human mathematics only after it is understood by humans.

Back to statement
3

A new academic system is needed: humans should not claim mathematical results they do not understand.

Read details
Details

The current system rewards people for stronger publications, but this system will not work when AI can directly produce strong mathematical results. We should reward humans who understand, communicate, and socialize mathematics.
